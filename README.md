Project Overview
Domain: Retail & E-Commerce Analytics

Tools Used: Microsoft Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX

Files Included: SuperStore_Sales_Dashboard.pbix

Key Business Insights
Top Sales Segment: Consumer segment contributes the highest sales share (~48%), followed by Corporate (33%) and Home Office (19%).

Payment Behavior: Cash on Delivery (COD) is the dominant checkout method (43%), followed by Online transactions (35%).

Category & Sub-Category Sales: Office Supplies and Technology generate the bulk of revenue, led by Phones and Chairs.

Operational Efficiency: Average order fulfillment turnaround is maintained at 4 days, with Standard Class being the primary shipping mode.

Technical Implementation
Data Transformation: Cleaned and prepared raw datasets using Power Query.

DAX Measures: Calculated YoY (Year-over-Year) growth for sales and profit trends.

Data Visualization: Built interactive KPI cards, bar charts, custom color palettes, and geographic slicers.
